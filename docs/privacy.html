Curiosity Mapped does not require you to provide your name, email address, or other directly identifying information simply to browse the website or use tools that operate entirely within your browser.

Curiosity Mapped does not need to receive or store information entered into a tool that operates entirely within your browser in order for that tool to perform its intended function.

However, using a browser-based tool does not necessarily mean that no information is collected about your visit. The webpage containing the tool may use analytics, advertising, security, or other third-party services that independently collect information about the visit.

If a particular tool requires information to be transmitted to a server, that tool may provide additional information about how the information is handled.

10. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on where you live and which services you use, you may have several ways to control information collection and use.

Analytics choice on this website

Every page of this website carries a Privacy choices control in the footer. It opens a panel where you can accept or decline Google Analytics, and it is also where you change or withdraw a choice you made earlier. The same panel appears on your first visit.

If you decline, Google Analytics does not read or write analytics cookies, any analytics cookies already stored are removed, and no further analytics information is sent from the page. If you accept, Google Analytics operates as described in the Google Analytics section above.

Before you have made a choice, your default depends on your location. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, analytics storage is denied by default, so no analytics cookies are used and no analytics identifier is created; a limited, cookieless signal containing information such as a timestamp, browser and device characteristics, the referring page, the page address, and your choice status may still be sent to Google. Elsewhere, analytics is enabled by default until you decline.

Your choice is stored in your browser’s local storage under the name cm-consent. It records only the choice itself and the date it was made; it contains no identifier and nothing that distinguishes you from any other visitor. It is never transmitted to Curiosity Mapped or to anyone else. The choice applies to the browser and device you made it on, and you will be asked again after twelve months, or sooner if you clear your browser storage.

Browser controls

Most modern browsers allow you to view, delete, block, or restrict cookies and similar technologies.

You may also use browser privacy controls, tracking protection, content blockers, or similar technologies.

Google Analytics

Google provides a Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on that can prevent Google Analytics from using information about your visits for Google Analytics measurement on supported websites.

Google advertising

If Google advertising is used, you may be able to manage certain advertising personalization preferences through Google’s advertising controls.
